# Environment Design Report

**Status: the world beyond V7's gate exists now, in all four languages; the opening sequence is complete; the later environments reuse what exists and are only partly developed.** 2026-11-06. Everything new is original and generated by script; no asset was downloaded.

## Before

V7's terrain is a 170 × 190 m slab, and nothing existed beyond it. From the storyboard's opening position, 120 m south of the gate, the camera looked at that slab floating in blank paper. A far wall stood as a flat band with a hard edge. North of the inner gate was an empty plain.

## What was built

| Element | How | Size | Languages |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The ground beyond V7** (`src/world.js`) | A procedural plain in every direction to 2.6 km, rising gently towards the mountains. **It meets V7's edges 15 cm below them,** using heights sampled every 2.5 m along all four edges (`assets/world/v7-terrain-edges.json`). Along the opening walk it stays low and quiet | 16.7k triangles | New ink rule (material 17): sparse long strokes that thin with distance, so the foreground stays quiet; paints like V7's terrain in memory and colour |
| **Three mountain ranges** | Rings of ridgelines at 750 m, 1.35 km and 2.3 km. Sharp crests from layered noise, with a craggy, asymmetric peak just west of north: **the far mountain, The Works** | 6.2k triangles (6,240) | Nearest range drawn in ink. **The two far ranges are not yet drawn in ink, only their outline** (material 16); warm haze in memory; cobalt, paler at the summits, in colour; black with a white edge at night |
| **Two monumental spires** | Tapered, slightly leaning, with two string courses and broken crowns; 62 m and 46 m, 330–370 m north-west of the gate | 384 triangles | Dark masses in every language |
| **The Witness Tree** (`pipeline/build_witness_tree.py`) | Grown by script: a leaning, twisting trunk, three main limbs splitting up to six times, six roots over the ground. Skin modifier, decimated, baked per vertex against V7's walls like every other asset | **9.0k triangles,** 8.1 m tall, 118 KB | V7's wood material: dark limbs in ink, warm wood in colour |
| **V7's far wall** | Hidden in the director | | |

**Total added, measured:** 32,285 triangles (ground 16,662, ranges 6,240, spires 384, tree 8,999) in 4 draw calls, plus 120 KB of download (the tree; the rest is generated at load). With the dummy and the far wall hidden, the frame is 409,428 triangles, against 391,078 before.

## Composition decisions

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **Spires west of the axis** | In the first try they stood behind V7's far ruin and vanished. Now gate, spires and ruin make a balanced triangle in the opening shot, with the gate between them |
| **The far mountain is withheld in ink** | Visible from the first frame, it killed the Revelation and read as a generic cone. In ink it is now only an outline, "not yet drawn"; colour reveals it. It also foreshadows the destination from the first frame. **Needs your approval** |
| **The far mountain moved west of north** | At the Revelation it had stood behind V7's far ruin. It now rises left of it, and the push-in frames it |
| **A quiet plain** | V7's terrain hatching, extended over a whole plain, turned into grey noise at grazing angles. The new plain is mostly paper, as a manga landscape leaves its foreground |
| **A dead tree, not a living one** | It reads strongly in ink as a silhouette, it has no thin leaves to shimmer, and it suits the "oldest living thing here, one bough still in leaf" idea once that bough is added |

## Visual research: Berserk manga and the 1997 anime

You asked me to use these as research only. I studied broad technique and **copied, traced or recreated nothing**: no background, panel, frame, symbol or recognisable scene. The compositions here are mine.

| Quality observed | Applied here |
|---|---|
| Huge painted skies with long, sweeping streaks of cirrus, cobalt deepening towards the zenith | A painted-cirrus layer in the colour sky (subtle so far: the existing cumulus still dominates) |
| Low camera and low horizon, tiny architecture against vast land and sky | The opening shot's language: 1.7 m eye height, the gate small under a big sky |
| Ranges layered by atmosphere: cooler, paler and less detailed with distance; light on the upper slopes | Mountain colour graded by distance and height; the far ranges withheld in ink |
| Manga landscapes leave broad blank paper, with sparse cracked-rock foreground marks | The plain's new sparse ink rule |
| Warm single-source light in dark interiors | Noted for the passage's abandoned camp; not built |

## What still needs your visual approval

1. The far mountain as an undrawn outline in ink, visible from the first frame.
2. The spires' placement and shape.
3. The Witness Tree's form and its place beside the arch.
4. The quieter ink of the open plain.
5. V7's far ruin: in colour it reads as a scribbled cobalt ghost, the weakest thing in the Revelation. It belongs to V7; replacing it is a decision for you.

## Known limitations

- The ground is coarse far away (cells up to about 200 m). From the walk it reads as rolling land, but a high camera would show facets.
- **No wind on the tree.**
- The far ranges' ink outline is the contour pass's; in places it breaks up at very long range.
- The cirrus is barely visible against the cumulus.
