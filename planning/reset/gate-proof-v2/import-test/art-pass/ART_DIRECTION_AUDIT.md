# Art Direction Audit

**Written for the owner, 6 November 2026.** What was wrong with the nine-world build as art, judged from its own captures against the references you supplied. Your screenshots were used to study technique only. None is used as a texture or background, and no composition or design is copied. The manga scans in `References/` were not opened.

## What the references do

| Quality | In the references |
|---|---|
| Sky | Clean blue gradient, long diagonal dry-brush cirrus, soft white masses with blue-grey undersides |
| Mountains | Pale planes with a light side and a shadow side, brushy edges, snow on the tops |
| Foliage | Watercolour leaf clusters with small dark accents; never a smooth ball |
| Grass | Long directional strokes with scattered dark scratches |
| Ink | Hatching that follows form, solid black masses, scratchy line, tone at distance, open paper |
| Figures | Few, placed with intent, secondary to the place |

## Findings

| Issue you listed | Muster Field before | Now, Muster Field only | Other eight worlds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oversized repetitive clouds | Pink scalloped shapes | **Fixed:** soft masses and cirrus. The masses are still too airbrushed | Still present |
| Flat, repetitive grass | Blotchy noise with identical tufts | **Partly:** directional strokes, but it stays one flat green at a distance | Still present |
| Generic tents | Cones and prisms | **Partly:** sagging panels, seams, guy ropes, pegs. Still simple shapes | Not applicable |
| Simplistic terrain | Almost level | **Not fixed** | Varies |
| Plastic-looking material | Smooth cel bands | **Partly:** brush variation on surfaces | Still present |
| Repeated tree shapes | Smooth lumps | **Partly:** leaf strokes, same silhouettes | Still present |
| Thin, inconsistent outlines | One weight everywhere | **Improved:** heavier silhouettes near the camera | Still present |
| Flat mountain silhouettes | Noisy stripes | **Improved:** light and shadow planes, snow | Still present |
| Excessively bright pastels | Pink and lilac | **Fixed in this world:** clear blue and restrained green | Still present in some |
| Generic NPCs | 16 animated mannequins | **Fixed:** the crowd is off by default | Few figures already |
| Insufficient detail | Empty surfaces | **Not fixed:** the biggest remaining weakness | Still present |

## Verdict

**The Muster Field is better, but it does not yet look like a painting.** Sky, mountains and palette moved furthest. The ink version moved further than the painted one. The middle ground of tents and grass still reads as a tidy stylised game, because its shapes are simple and clean. A shader cannot add what the shapes lack. See `NEXT_STAGE_RECOMMENDATION.md`.
