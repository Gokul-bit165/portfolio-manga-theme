# Next Stage Recommendation

**Written for the owner, 6 November 2026.** Nothing beyond the Muster Field has changed. The other worlds wait for your decision.

## Recommendation: one more pass on the Muster Field, before any other world

**Don't roll this out yet.** The system works and the performance has room, but the picture isn't at the level of your references, and copying it to eight more worlds would copy its weaknesses. In order of expected gain:

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lower, warmer sun with long cast shadows** | The bright, even light removed the drama. The old baseline's long shadows were better. Probably the largest gain for the least work |
| 2 | **Near-frame foreground:** branches, rocks or a ruined wall across the frame | The references compose in layers; this shot has almost no foreground |
| 3 | **A stronger composition:** a real subject and a lower camera in place of the wide, level view down a road | The frame has no focal point. Each shot should work as a still |
| 4 | **Better shapes in the middle ground:** irregular tents, stacked gear, varied trees | The shader can't add what the shapes lack. Your request to reuse licensed assets belongs here |
| 5 | **Solid blacks and hatch scale in ink:** line spacing that grows near the camera, large black masses | Fixes the moiré and gives ink its weight |
| 6 | **Firmer, more painted cloud masses** | They look airbrushed |
| 7 | **Separate words and memories from figures** | So the world's text can return with the crowd off, and the large HTML plate can go |

## Then

Apply the agreed treatment world by world, starting with the worlds it suits most: Vael for painted colour, the Quiet Camp for dark ink.

## Decisions for you

1. **Which direction leads?** B (ink) is the strongest today. I'd make ink the main language of the plain and of memories, and keep painted colour for selected worlds.
2. **Do the one-more-pass above first?** I recommend yes.
3. **Should I look for licensed medieval assets for the middle ground?** I recommend yes, CC0 first, starting with the Quaternius Medieval Village kit named in the plan. Downloads need your go-ahead.
