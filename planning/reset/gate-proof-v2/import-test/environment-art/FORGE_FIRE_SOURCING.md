# Forge fire: sourcing hand-painted 2D fire animation

Research for the owner's brief of 2026-11-07, "Final Forge fire art direction". The question: is there professionally authored, hand-painted 2D fire animation we may legally use, at enough resolution for the final camera zoom?

## What the final zoom needs

- **Size:** the fire fills a 1600 x 900 frame and spans about 4 m by 2 m.
- **Resolution:** frames need at least 1024 px across the whole fire, or about 512 px per tongue if tongues are separate.
- **Animation:** 8–16 frames, looping, ideally with separate layers or several tongue variants.
- **Licence:** the files must be allowed on a public website, which serves them to every visitor.

## What can make the fire the brief asks for

1. **Buy a hand-drawn pack.** The ducvu pack is the lead, after the owner reads its licence and frame size. Frames under about 512 px would still be too soft for the zoom.
2. **Draw it in Blender Grease Pencil.** A person draws 12–16 frames of the whole fire as one drawing, in three layers (outer, body, core), over the fireplace scan seen from the zoom camera. I can prepare that Blender file: camera, reference, layers, onion skin, palette, frame range. I can also write the export that turns the drawing into the runtime's atlas, so the art goes in unchanged. **I cannot draw it.** Any shape I make is generated by code, which the brief rules out.
3. **Commission an animator** for 12–16 frames to the same specification.

## Built so the art can drop in

Whichever source is chosen, the runtime will use one atlas for both the normal view and the final zoom, at full resolution with mipmaps. That avoids a visibly different close-up and the B-to-C dissolve.

- **Planned layout:** the whole fire drawn as one image per frame, about 1024 x 512, in three mask channels. Twelve frames make a 4096 x 1536 atlas: about 25 MB of GPU memory with mipmaps, or about 8 MB in a GPU-compressed format.
- **Compression caveat:** a compressed format needs a KTX2 or Basis loader, which is a new dependency and would need approval.
- **Current drawn fire:** its two textures use about 24 MB of GPU memory.
