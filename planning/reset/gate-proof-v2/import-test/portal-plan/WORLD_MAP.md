# World Map

**Status: proposal for your approval. Nothing here is built.** Nine original worlds connected by portals, one per portfolio chapter, drawn like a 1990s painted anime moved into 3D.

## The line this plan holds

This is an **original world**, not the Berserk universe. It does not include Guts, Griffith, Casca, Puck, the Band of the Hawk, Doldrey, the Eclipse, the brand, or any location, emblem, costume or scene someone could identify as coming from the series. The downloaded Guts model is not used. "Private" does not change this for me, and it matters practically too: this is meant to become your public portfolio, so everything built here can ship.

**What stays from your brief:** the structure, the portals, medieval dark-fantasy scale, the painted 2D-in-3D look, living environments, and every purpose you gave each world. Castles, battlefields, forges, mercenary camps and mountain roads belong to history and to the whole genre, so they are all available.

**Test for every design:** could someone name a specific work it came from? If yes, redesign it.

## The world's own story

A long road crosses a broken kingdom. **The Red Line,** the surveyor's cord already running through the First Gate, is the thread: each world is a place the line reaches, and every portal is an opening in masonry, rock, timber or sky that the line passes through. It says nothing about you; your facts appear only in the content.
