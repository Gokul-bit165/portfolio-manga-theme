## What works

- **The First Gate** (p 0.42–0.52): memory seen through the arch, ink around it. Original, specific, and it could not be done with a filter.
- **The tilt-up** (p 0.49): the arch fills the frame at the moment it changes the world.
- **The Witness Tree in ink** (p 0.27–0.33): a strong silhouette, grown by script.
- **The night gate** (p 0.97–1.0): the black shape with a thin white line.
- **Passage in memory** (p 0.52–0.70): rich, warm, and the most painterly material here.
