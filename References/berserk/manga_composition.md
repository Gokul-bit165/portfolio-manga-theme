# Manga Composition

Tags: **[O]** observed, **[M]** measured, **[I]** interpretation. Citation format is in the [README](README.md#citation-format).

**Reading direction:** these volumes read right to left (the "STOP" page at v16 p228 explains this). Eye-movement notes below assume that. A left-to-right or vertical portfolio must mirror or re-plan any flow it learns from.

## Pacing: density followed by rest

The strongest compositional habit is alternation.

- **[O]** A page can be nearly empty: v03 p176 is a small line figure on blank paper, and v13 p49 has one small off-centre panel on a white page.
- **[O]** Next to dense pages, it is a rest. v21 p166 and v19 p70 are packed from edge to edge.
- **[O]** Sequences step down after intense pages. v13 p202 runs from sky strips to quiet plants, then to a tiny body in a burst. v14 p31 then opens onto clean cumulus clouds and a rural scene.
- **[M]** 5.8% of Conviction pages are over 35% near-black, and another 5.8% are over 70% near-white. Most pages sit between them, and the extremes act as punctuation.
- **[I]** Rest is not filler. It makes the following density feel heavier. A portfolio with nine dark full-screen sections would lose this, whereas one quiet screen could do more than another piece of art.

## Scale: extremes instead of middle distance

- **[O]** Extreme close-ups: one eye filling half a page (v22 p86), a mask's eye (v03 p149).
- **[O]** Extreme wide shots: a marsh spread with a tiny flying figure (v15 p172), a tower above a plain (v34 p6).
- **[O]** Close-up and extreme wide often share a page. v22 p38 sets two close-up faces beside a long pale column with two tiny figures.
- **[O]** Scale figures appear in nearly every architectural view: v06 p148, v14 p211, v29 p168, v33 p96.
- **[O]** Crowds become texture: v07 p184, v17 p102, v32 p66, v34 p36.
- **[O]** Huge subjects often sit behind tiny ones with nothing in between: v20 p166 has a giant hand on a black hill above a mass of faces.
- **[I]** Medium shots carry dialogue. Emotional and environmental peaks use the extremes. Gothic Study V3's medium view of the arch is the least characteristic distance. See [blender_translation.md](blender_translation.md).

## Panel patterns

| Pattern | What it does | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Stacked horizontal strips | Controlled zoom or time passing | [O] v03 p149, v17 p102, v13 p67 |
| Zoom ladder | Same subject, closer each strip, ending on an eye | [O] v03 p149 |
| Tall vertical panels split one view | One place seen in slices, slows the eye | [O] v22 p7, v33 p96 |
| Skewed narrow slivers on black | Panels themselves express panic and distance | [O] v13 p175 |
| Full bleed behind small panels | Small human moments placed on a huge landscape | [O] v01 p151, v21 p166 |
| Figure breaks the panel | Force, or a figure too large for the frame | [O] v01 p91, v14 p166 |
| Black gutters or margins | The whole page feels like night | [O] v33 p141, v21 p166 |
| Panel as a shaped vignette | The image shape itself becomes a cave | [O] v20 p86 |
| Single small panel on empty page | Silence | [O] v13 p49, v06 p76 |
| Chapter opener on black | A breath, with a small title | [O] v24 p102, v26 p24, v32 p96, v34 p96 |

Two details stand out:

- **[O]** v13 p175 shrinks its panels in steps until they are thin slits in black. The page appears to close.
- **[O]** v17 p102 shows the same scene in three identical small panels at the bottom, so that only the mass changes.

## Camera angles

| Angle | Effect | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Sharply upward with converging verticals | Buildings loom | [O] v02 p199, v33 p141 |
| Low, near the ground | Figure dominates, viewer is small | [O] v07 p157, v14 p166, v17 p186 |
| High, looking down | Tactical, people become small | [O] v01 p11, v07 p202, v29 p114 |
| One-point perspective down a corridor | Draws the eye far in, then stops it | [O] v06 p148, v33 p141 |
| Strict frontal and symmetrical | Monumental, ritual, confrontation | [O] v02 p190, v17 p60, v33 p96 |
| Long diagonal | Movement and journey | [O] v29 p168 |
| Tilted frame | Instability in action or strain | [O] v28 p78 |

- **[I]** Upward views are not corrected to parallel verticals. The convergence is part of the drama. V2 and V3 used lens shift to keep verticals straight, which is the architectural catalogue habit your brief wanted to avoid.

## Foreground and background

- **[O]** Dark or plain near shapes push space back. On v06 p148 a large bald head at the right frames a deep hall. On v29 p96 statues frame a building. v26 p132 frames a burning building with black tree shapes.
- **[O]** Often near is dark and far is pale, in clear bands: v01 p151, v22 p166.
- **[O]** The reverse also happens, with black shapes in front of bright light: v24 p150, where black figures stand against a bright doorway.
- **[O]** Backgrounds may be dropped when the emotion matters more. v01 p91 replaces the background with a flat tone shape, and v17 p60 has none.
- **[I]** The background is treated as a choice, not a duty. Detail appears when place matters, and goes when feeling matters.

## Negative space

- **[O]** White space as emptiness: v23 p102 has a small pale form on a curved horizon under a large tone gradient. v22 p38 uses a pale column to hold distance between two tiny figures.
- **[O]** Black space as stage: v15 p46, with pale forms hanging in black. v17 p60 is the same idea with a face. v23 p182 uses star-filled black over solid silhouettes.
- **[I]** Both kinds are used without apology for pages at a time. Negative space carries meaning here: distance, loss, or awe.

## Large dramatic panels

- **[O]** Full pages and spreads appear at turning points, and usually have one clear read: figure against mass (v01 p91), silhouettes against sky (v23 p182), or a huge form (v20 p166).
- **[M]** Spreads rise from 3.1% of held Golden Age pages to 9.1% of Falcon pages. The file structure differs between early and late volumes, so treat this as approximate.
- **[I]** A big panel works because of the small panels before it. The pages leading to v01 p91 are busy, so the full figure lands harder.

## Eye movement

- **[O]** Bright shapes in dark areas attract first: an eye (v22 p86), a white balloon (v17 p60), a raised blade (v15 p46).
- **[O]** Lines of objects lead the eye: bow rhythms (v32 p66), footprints (v22 p38), a wall's diagonal (v29 p168), and street perspective (v33 p141).
- **[O]** Sound effect lettering is often large and part of the composition, crossing the figure (v01 p91, v21 p118, v28 p24).
- **[O]** Speech balloons are pale shapes placed in dark areas, where they also function as composition (v28 p24).
- **[I]** Attention goes where the local contrast is highest, so text placement and composition are the same decision.

## Lessons for a vertical scrolling site

**[I]** All of this section is translation, not observation.

| Source device | Scrolling equivalent |
|---|---|
| Long column between two tiny figures (v22 p38) | A tall screen where scroll distance *is* the journey, marked by a line of tracks |
| Zoom ladder (v03 p149) | Scrolled sequence of tighter crops, ending on one detail |
| Panels shrink into black (v13 p175) | Section exit where panels narrow and the page closes to ink |
| Chapter opener on black (v24 p102) | One quiet screen with a small title before each chapter |
| Near-blank page (v03 p176) | Generous empty screen after a dense project gallery |
| Slices of one view (v22 p7) | Tall vertical panels of one environment for project intros |
| Crowd as rhythm (v32 p66) | Repeated marks as texture or dividers |

Native scroll is preserved in all of these. Nothing needs hijacking, only tall layouts and restrained triggers.

## What must not be recreated

Use the principles, not the layouts. Do not rebuild these specific pages: v01 p91, v03 p149, v13 p175, v15 p46, v17 p60, v22 p38, v23 p182. Each new layout needs its own subject, proportions and shapes.
