# Portfolio Vision

## Concept
**"THE JOURNEY OF AN ENGINEER — A STORY TOLD THROUGH INK."** A vertical manga-style story where scrolling is turning pages. A lone wandering engineer-warrior (original character) travels through a dark medieval world; a tiny playful fairy-like companion provides contrast and humor. Each chapter reveals a facet of Gokul's real work.

## Reference mood (atmosphere only)
Gothic fantasy, dense cross-hatched ink, heavy blacks, parchment, cinematic panel composition. **Not** copying: no Berserk panels, logos, named characters, armor/sword silhouettes, or recognizable designs. Guts/Puck = broad contrast idea (grim vs. playful) only.

## Original character brief (ASSUMPTION — needs approval, D3)
- **The Wanderer:** hooded traveler with a satchel, a heavy tool-staff/lantern instead of a giant sword, patched travel cloak, face often in shadow. Silhouette must differ from any Berserk character. Carries a laptop-like "grimoire" motif subtly.
- **The Spark:** small luminous companion (moth/lantern-sprite style, not Puck's elf design), expressive, comments with short mono captions.

## Narrative arc
Dark/monochrome uncertainty → sepia growth and craft → full color when the work is shown and shared. Colour = earned understanding; mapping in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

## Goals
1. Memorable, original first impression within 5 seconds.
2. Recruiter can find projects, skills, achievements, contact in under 30 seconds regardless of story (skip nav + chapter index).
3. Story supports the content; never obstructs it.
4. Fast, accessible, mobile-capable.

## Non-goals
Game-like mechanics, horizontal scroll, scroll-jacking, auto-playing sound, copying existing IP, fake metrics.

## Anti-goals (failure even if it "works")
- Reads as a fan site or game page rather than a professional editorial portfolio.
- Text unreadable over art; heavy load/jank on mid-range phones.
- Generic template look, glow/gradient slop, repeated identical card grids.
- Any unverified claim about Gokul.

## Tone of voice
Measured, direct, slightly literary headings; plain technical body text; mono labels for metadata. Humor only via the companion. [NEEDS CONFIRMATION: owner's preferred voice sample]

## Assumptions
- Story is decorative framing; real content is always in semantic HTML, not baked into images.
- Sound is off; optional later.
